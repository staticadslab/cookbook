`../llm-wiki` (GitHub: `staticadslab/llm-wiki`).

- Before starting work: pull `../llm-wiki` and read `../llm-wiki/wiki/index.md` plus
  `../llm-wiki/wiki/products/cookbook.md`.

  `../llm-wiki/schema/AGENTS.md`.
